#### Task 112
> **Question/Prompt:**  Search and identify LED0, a LED that you will program to lit later.

> **Answer/Explanation:**
> LED0 corresponds to the PC7 pin on the board, also known as the USER LED.

---

#### Task 113
> **Question/Prompt:**  Search and identify SW0, a switch that you will program later to be interpreted as pressed/not pressed.

> **Answer/Explanation:**
> SW0 corresponds to PC6, which is also known as the USER BUTTON.

---

#### Task 114
> **Question/Prompt:**   Search and identify other components like external oscillator and debugger chip/microcontroller.

> **Answer/Explanation:**
> The external oscillator is represented by the 16MHz crystal oscilaltor and the debugger chip is represented by the embedded debugger (EDBG).
