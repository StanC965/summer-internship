119 - OPTIONAL - Even though there is no external power supply connected to VCC and GND,
the board is still powered up (green LED lit) when plugging in the USB cable. Why?
Answer: The USB cable itself provides power to the board. The onboard voltage regulator takes this USB voltage and supplies the board,
so no separate external power source is needed.
