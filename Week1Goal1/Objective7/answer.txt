Objective 7 - Basic electronics

181 - How to connect a LED in a circuit
A LED is a diode: it emits light only under direct polarization (+ on the anode, - on the cathode)
The long leg is the anode (+), the short leg is the cathode (-)
A resistor must be placed in series with the LED to limit the current, otherwise the LED
would draw maximum current and burn out
Ohm's law: I = U / R
