What pin is OC0A?
PB3

Which LED is connected to it?
I/O board LED

What do you observe with respect to the two LEDs blinking behavior?
The two LEDs are blinking out of sync. One LED turns on at the moment the other turns off.

How much real time (use ms/miliseconds) can be measured with OCR0A = 127?
With a prescale of 1024: 131.072 ms
