\# Week 1 - Goal 1 - Objective 2



\## Knowing your extension board: OLED1



Important note: the OLED1 extension board must not be connected to the main board while the USB cable is plugged in. The USB cable must be unplugged first.



\## 121

The OLED display is the small black display placed on the OLED1 Xplained Pro board.



\## 122

LED1, LED2 and LED3 are the three user LEDs placed under the OLED display. These LEDs will be controlled later from software.



\## 123

BUTTON 1, BUTTON 2 and BUTTON 3 are the three push buttons placed under LED1, LED2 and LED3. These buttons will be used later as digital inputs.



\## 124

The extension header is the black connector placed at the top side of the OLED1 board. It is used to connect the OLED1 board to the main ATmega324PB Xplained Pro board.



\## 125

The GND pins are marked on the OLED1 board near the board corners and on the extension connector. The VCC pin is part of the extension header and provides the supply voltage for the board.



\## 126

To connect the OLED1 board safely, I followed this procedure:



1\. Unplug the USB cable from the PC/laptop.

2\. Connect the OLED1 board to the EXT1 header of the main board.

3\. Reconnect the USB cable to the PC/laptop.



This procedure must always be followed before connecting an extension board to the main board, to avoid damaging the boards.
