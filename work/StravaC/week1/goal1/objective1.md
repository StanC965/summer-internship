\# Week 1 - Goal 1 - Objective 1



\## 111

The main microcontroller on the board is ATmega324PB.



\## 112

LED0 is the user LED on the main board, placed near SW0. It will be controlled from software through PC7.



\## 113

SW0 is the user push button on the main board.



\## 114

Other important components on the board are the RESET button, USB connector, JTAG/debug connector, power pins, extension headers and the on-board debugger/programming circuitry.



\## 115

The board has 4 extension headers.



\## 116

The extension headers are labeled EXT1, EXT2, EXT3 and EXT4.



\## 117

GND and VCC/3.3V pins are marked on the board and on the extension headers.



\## 118

The board can be powered through the USB connector.



\## 119

The board powers on through USB because the USB cable provides power from the PC/laptop. The board power circuitry distributes this supply to the required voltage rails.
