334 - BMW central control panel

Design:
- panel.c/h is a new module on the application level. It keeps the panel state
  (UNBLOCKED / BLOCKED) and the logic of the 3 vents.

Behavior:
- UNBLOCKED: each button toggles only its own vent LED.
  		Button X only touches LED X.
- BLOCKED: LED0 is on, the vent LEDs stay off, and any button press fast blinks LED0
  to tell the driver that the panel is blocked.
- SW0 pressed again -> back to UNBLOCKED.
