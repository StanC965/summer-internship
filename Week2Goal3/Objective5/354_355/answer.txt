354 - observations:
On my board the value is inverted:
- bright light (phone flashlight on the sensor): about 0x03
- room light: about 0xE6
- covered (dark): about 0xF5
So the ADC reacts over the whole range. With VREF = 1.1V the ADC saturates at 0xFF for
the higher inputs, because the sensor voltage goes above the 1.1V reference and the ADC
cannot represent voltages larger than its reference

355 - design rules:
Rule 1 - every module has an init function:
  gpio_init(), led_init(), button_init(), sos_init(), adc_init(), panel_init()
  gpio and sos have no internal state, so their init is empty
Rule 2 - atomic driver actions as dedicated functions:
  gpio driver: gpio_set_pin, gpio_reset_pin, gpio_toggle_pin, gpio_read_pin,
               gpio_set_direction, gpio_enable_pullup
  adc driver : adc_start_conversion (start), adc_get_result (get the result)
