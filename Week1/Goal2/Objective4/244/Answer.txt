In my opinion the guardians have the role of preventing the files they are in from being included multiple times during the compilation process.
if header files are included multiple times they can cause redefinition errors,so guardians check if they aare already defined to prevent that from happening.
When gpio.h is included once, it's contents appear once both with and without guardians.
When it's included twice, it's contents appear only once with guardians and duplicated, one after the other without guardians.
