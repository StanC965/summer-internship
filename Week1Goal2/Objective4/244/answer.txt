244 - Role of the include guards (#ifndef GPIO_H / #define GPIO_H / #endif)

Role: they prevent a header from being included more than once in the same compiled file.
The first include defines GPIO_H and processes the content, a second include finds GPIO_H
already defined, so the content is skipped.

Comparison of the .i files:

                          | gpio.h included ONCE          | gpio.h included TWICE
--------------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------
gpio.h WITH guards        | content appears 1x, compiles  | content appears 1x (2nd skipped),
                          |                               | compiles OK
--------------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------
gpio.h WITHOUT guards     | content appears 1x, compiles  | content appears 2x in the .i,
                          |                               | but still compiles in my case

It WOULD give an error if the header contained DEFINITIONS that cannot be duplicated,
such as a variable definition, a function body, or a typedef/struct definition.
Then including it twice without guards produces "redefinition" errors.
