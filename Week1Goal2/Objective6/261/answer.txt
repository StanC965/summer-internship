261 - SOS signal

SOS in Morse: . . . - - - . . . (3 points, 3 lines, 3 points)

Software architecture of the system:

Abstraction Level        | Elements
-------------------------|--------------------------------------------
Application software     | sos.c, sos.h
Intermediate software    | led.c, led.h, button.c, button.h
Basic software  	 | gpio.c, gpio.h, iom324pb.h
Hardware                 | microcontroller registers (PORTC, DDRC, PINC)
Physical                 | voltage levels (GND, VCC)

Each level only talks to the level below it.
