433 - CTC waveform with ~100ms period on OC0A (PB3 = LED4).

period = 2 * match_interval = 2 * (OCR0A+1) * prescaler / 1MHz
OCR0A = 195, prescaler = 256 -> match_interval = 196 * 256us = 50.176 ms
period = 2 * 50.176 = 100.352 ms  (~100 ms, ~10 Hz)

Is this observable with the human eye?
Yes. At ~10 Hz the LED is clearly seen flickering fast
